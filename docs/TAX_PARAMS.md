# TY2025 and TY2026 federal individual income tax parameters

Researched 2026-11-18. All figures verified against primary sources unless marked **UNVERIFIED (memory)**. The 2025 column is post-OBBBA where OBBBA changed a figure. QSS means Qualifying Surviving Spouse and uses the MFJ figure everywhere.

**Sources:**
- RP24-40: Rev. Proc. 2024-40, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-24-40.pdf (text extracted locally)

- IRC: statute text at law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/{section}, as amended by P.L. 119-21

## 17. Capital losses, wash sales, Section 1256

- **Capital loss limit:** $3,000 against ordinary income, or $1,500 for MFS (sec. 1211(b)). The carryforward text was not checked. **UNVERIFIED (memory):** unused losses carry forward indefinitely and keep their short-term or long-term character (sec. 1212(b)).
- **Wash sale:** the window runs 30 days before through 30 days after the sale, 61 days in total (sec. 1091(a)).
- **Section 1256:** gains and losses are 60% long-term and 40% short-term regardless of holding period. Open contracts are marked to market on the last business day of the year. Nonequity options (listed options that are not equity options) are included (sec. 1256(a), (b), (g)(3)). Whether a given index option qualifies depends on the product being a nonequity option. Specific products were not checked.

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1211, /1091 and /1256
